About me

A developing technical portfolio grounded in real-world delivery

My background spans project management, operations, business support and education. I currently work in project delivery in the pharmaceutical packaging and clinical-trials sector, where accuracy, communication and controlled processes matter every day.

Alongside work, I am completing a part-time MSc in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence. I created this portfolio to show not only finished work, but also how my thinking, technical ability and confidence develop throughout the degree.

I enjoy finding ways to make things work better, whether that means organising a project, untangling a process or finding a clearer way to share information. My professional experience has made me curious about how technology could help with the everyday challenges people face at work.

Studying Computer Science with AI is giving me the opportunity to turn that curiosity into practical skills. I am particularly interested in how software, data and AI can reduce repetitive tasks and give people more time to focus on work that needs their judgement and expertise.

I would like to build on my experience and knowledge to create solutions that help people rather than leave it behind. This portfolio is a space to share that progression: what I am learning, what I can create and how I can apply it. I am proud of where I have started, and excited to see where these skills take me.
