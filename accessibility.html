Accessibility statement

Last updated: September 2026

I want this portfolio to be usable by as many people as possible and will continue improving it as my knowledge develops.

Measures included

  • Semantic headings and page landmarks.
  • A keyboard-accessible skip link.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators.
  • Responsive layouts that reflow on smaller screens.
  • Colour combinations designed for readable contrast.
  • Descriptive link text and alternative text for meaningful images.

Known limitations

Some future project documents or third-party services may not be fully controlled by me. Any known issue will be documented and improved where reasonably possible.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact me through the contact page and explain the page and problem encountered.
